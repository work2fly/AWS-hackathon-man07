# Free 3D Model Resources for Female Therapist Avatar

## 🎭 Best Sources for Free Models

### 1. Ready Player Me ⭐ (Easiest)
**URL**: https://readyplayer.me/

**Pros**:
- Free, no account needed
- Customizable avatars
- Professional quality
- Direct GLB download
- Perfect for therapy context

**How to use**:
1. Visit the website
2. Create a custom avatar
3. Choose professional clothing
4. Download as GLB
5. Use the URL: `https://models.readyplayer.me/[AVATAR_ID].glb`

**Example URL**:
```
https://models.readyplayer.me/6571e4e3c3e5e5f3e3e5e5f3.glb
```

## 🎨 Recommended Models for Therapy Context

### Professional & Approachable
Look for models with:
- ✅ Professional clothing (blazer, business casual)
- ✅ Friendly facial expression
- ✅ Neutral pose
- ✅ Age: 30-50 range
- ✅ Glasses (optional, adds professionalism)

### Avoid
- ❌ Fantasy/game characters
- ❌ Overly casual clothing
- ❌ Extreme poses
- ❌ Cartoon/anime style (unless that's your aesthetic)

---

## 📦 Model Specifications

For best performance, choose models with:

| Specification | Recommended | Maximum |
|---------------|-------------|---------|
| File Size | < 2MB | < 5MB |
| Polygons | 10,000-30,000 | 50,000 |
| Textures | 512x512 | 1024x1024 |
| Format | GLB | GLB/GLTF |
| Rigged | Yes (optional) | - |
